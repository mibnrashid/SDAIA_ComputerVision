What this shows

A computer never sees a cup: it receives 576 numbers. Each step blurs less: step 2 averages 4 × 4 blocks, step 3 averages 2 × 2 blocks, step 4 shows every pixel at low contrast and step 5 shows the real 24 × 24 image. Recognising the object from the numbers alone is the problem the whole course solves (slide 1.14, the semantic gap).
