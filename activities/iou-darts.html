Scoring

Four throws at IoU 0.3, 0.5, 0.75 and 0.9. Hitting the target exactly scores 10; every 0.02 you are off costs 1 point (0.2 off or more scores 0). IoU = overlap area ÷ combined area (slide 2.20) — most people are surprised how much overlap 0.5 needs.
